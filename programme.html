The Programme

Four Weeks.
Deep Work.

A blended structure of pre-recorded sessions and live weekend classes, built for those who are AI-native and ready to move beyond surface-level use.

01
Week One

AI Foundations & Mental Models

Understand how large language models work — not just what they produce, but why, and where they fail.

02
Week Two

Prompt Engineering & Critical Evaluation

Move from accepting AI outputs to shaping and interrogating them. Detect hallucinations, bias, and gaps.

03
Week Three

AI Across Industries

Apply AI across creative, business, technical, and social sectors — with a Nigerian workforce lens built in.

04
Week Four

Capstone & Career Application

Ship a real project demonstrating depth, not just fluency. Build a portfolio piece you can defend and explain.

What's Included
  • Pre-recorded weekly deep-dive sessions
  • Live weekend workshop sessions
  • Practical assignments each module
  • One-on-one mentoring sessions
  • Capstone project with real-world brief
  • Certificate of completion
  • Alumni community access
